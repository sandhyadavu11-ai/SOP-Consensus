# S&OP Consensus Prototype — Vitala Beverage Line

A working prototype of a **Sales & Operations Planning consensus view** for one
product line, built to test a single claim: *when demand, capacity, inventory,
and financial data sit in one model, decisions surface that nobody could see
while the data lived in four separate spreadsheets.*

## The story

Vitala is a functional-beverage line: 8 SKUs, 2 bottling plants, 3 distribution
centers, 3 channels. Like most mid-size CPG operations, its planning data lives
in silos — a demand planner's stat forecast, a sales team's commitment, a plant
scheduler's capacity sheet, and finance's annual operating plan (AOP). Each
spreadsheet looks fine on its own. Joined together in one semantic model, four
decisions appear that no single view could show:

| # | What the consensus view surfaces | The decision it forces |
|---|----------------------------------|------------------------|
| 1 | Q4-2026 promo demand runs ~120% of combined plant capacity | Pre-build inventory in Aug–Sep, or cap the promotion — deciding in October is too late |
| 2 | Sales forecast carries a persistent **+10–11% bias** vs. actual shipments | Which number does supply plan against? Planning on the sales number ties up ~$2M in working capital; planning on stat risks shorting the promo |
| 3 | Melon Hydrate (19% margin) sits at **130+ days of supply** while Citrus Boost (52% margin) fills only **~92%** of demand | Shift line time from legacy melon batches to the hero SKU — a pure margin-mix win invisible unless inventory, margin, and service sit side by side |
| 4 | H2-2026 consensus revenue lands **−6% vs. AOP** | Surface the gap in May, not at year-end — and evaluate levers (promo, mix, price) with their capacity cost attached |

## What's here

```
data/                  8 CSVs — monthly grain, Jan 2024 – Jun 2027, actuals through Aug 2026
scripts/generate_data.py   Synthetic data generator (seeded, pure Python) with the four
                           tension points deliberately baked in and self-validated on run
scripts/build_report_page.py  Writes the Consensus One-Pager dashboard (10 visuals) directly
                              into the report definition — the reconciled four-view page
docs/report_build_guide.md Visual-by-visual walkthrough for the 3-page Power BI report
docs/model_reference.md    Semantic model documentation: tables, relationships, all DAX measures
```

The Power BI semantic model is a clean star schema (4 dimensions, 4 facts)
with ~20 DAX measures in four display folders — Demand, Supply, Inventory,
Financial — including forecast bias/accuracy, capacity utilization, projected
inventory and days of supply, gap-to-AOP, and revenue-at-risk (unconstrained
demand vs. capacity-constrained supply, priced).

## Rebuild from scratch

1. `python scripts/generate_data.py` — regenerates the CSVs and prints a
   validation block confirming all four tensions hold in the data.
2. Open the `.pbix` (or follow `docs/model_reference.md` to rebuild the model)
   and refresh.
3. Page 1 (the consensus dashboard) is generated by `python scripts/build_report_page.py`
   (run with Desktop closed); pages 2–3 are assembled per `docs/report_build_guide.md`.

## Why one product line

The prototype deliberately scopes to a single line. The point isn't coverage —
it's proving the consensus mechanism fast enough to earn the right to scale it.
Every design choice (monthly grain, star schema, forecast-version columns,
measure folders) extends directly to a full portfolio.
